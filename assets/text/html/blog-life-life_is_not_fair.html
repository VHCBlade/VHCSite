Life isn't Fair

Whenever I remember my High School days, the catch phrase of one of my friends always rings in my ears. She'd take any opportunity to say it– usually when someone would complain about life not being fair.

"In life you don't always get what you want! I wanted to memorize the dictionary when I was younger, but in life you don't always get what you want!" Her self-satisfied grin as she said it is still burned into my brain.

Yes, she'd say that whole spiel every time. No, it was never abridged.

Now you might be thinking, "Haron, that's not a very good catchphrase. It's way too long and won't come up often." Yet somehow, I heard it over and over again, pretty much every time we met. It was catchy enough that I still hear it in my head.

And it was so meaningful that it still sticks with me to this day.

We are Not Made Equal

Some people believe that God made us all equal– with equal gifts and equal potential. It seems like a nice and fair rule in our supposedly egalitarian society.

This is not true.

God made some people stronger, smarter, healthier, and yes even HOLIER than others. He gives each one of us different blessings.

Take the "newly" canonized Saint Carlo Acutis.

At such a young age, he was already updating websites for his Church.

This website was down for several months because I couldn't be bothered to update it. (SSL expired)

Through the website Carlo updated, as well as by the friendships he forged, he was able to bring about the conversion and reversions of the multitude of people he interacted with.

I only have the reversion of myself to my name. (And even that is questionable)

Acutis faced an early death to Leukemia with courage and a fire to drive him to be as holy as possible.

My complaints from a common cold would make you think that I had been terminally ill.

God gave Saint Carlo graces that are different from the graces that he gave me. And that's OK!

In the parable of the talents, the master doesn't expect 10 talents from the one he gave 3 talents to. He expects that of the one he gave 5 talents to. Though when comparing myself to Carlo Acutis, it feels like his talents are a thousandfold of mine.

And Saint Carlo's multitude of graces are no reason for me to be envious of him. The graces that I receive are wholly sufficient for me to be holy– to be the saint that God made me to be. And rather than look at how "unfair" God and the universe is for making some people better than me, I should instead celebrate the many blessings in my life.

And the same is true for you too. God has made you for great things.

And when we give our life to God, he will shroud us with the love that we need to be great.

Life is not a Competition

But what does it mean to be great?

If I were to take up poetry, would I need to rival Shakespeare to be great?

Would I need to contribute to the level of Alan Turing to earn my stripes as a programmer?

Do I need to become president of the Philippines to become a good Filipino Citizen?

Of course not!

If that were true, then 99.9999999999999% of the human race would be catastrophic unsalvageable failures. However, if we open our eyes and look around, we'll see that's clearly not true. If you have loving parents, they would seem irreplaceable. If you have friends, you'd thank God that you have them. If you've ever had a teacher or a speaker that has made an impact in your life, you'd think that that alone would have made their lives worthwhile.

We are all going to die… And most people will be forgotten within a few generations. I'm sure that most of us don't know the names of our great great grandparents. (Though if I look through some photos of our cemetery visits, I could probably tell you mine)

Only a handful of people are lucky enough to be remembered. However, we can still do great things in our day to day lives.

Because every single human life is precious and worth the world. So all we have to do is to make the lives of those close to us better than it was yesterday. Because every soul that we touch, that we care for… that we save… writes our name in heaven's book… including when we save our own. Because before we were even born, we were already loved, by Love Himself. And when we love closer to that perfect love, we ourselves become great.

And that's all it takes!

Little by little…

We become great…

And then we begin to see the truth:

Our Weaknesses are not an Accident

Sometimes we think that our weaknesses shouldn't make us… weak. That the difficulties we face shouldn't be… difficult. That the suffering we face won't make us… suffer. We often have a strained relationship with the English language.

If the pains in life weren't painful, it wouldn't be pain now would it?

Every time we're tempted, we buckle, and we fall, we're tempted to think that we're irredeemable… useless… broken.

Wherever we are… Whether it's high on life and crushing every challenge– or down and struggling with the same difficulties as yesterday. Whether it's finishing first– or tripping at every single hurdle and stone… That's where God wants you to be.

It is through our very human weaknesses that God slowly perfects us.

Like the bellowing fire of a forge.

Like the chisel forming the stone.

These hurdles, that God allows to come our way, shape and mold us to become more like Him. Because our trust should not be in anything in this world. Not in our own accomplishments nor the accomplishments of any other person. Because in this world, nothing will satisfy us. There will be no perfect bliss, no happy ending. We will always have a cross to carry.

It is only when we are made perfect and ready for heaven that we will no longer struggle. But while in this world, embrace your suffering for the blessing it is. Because through it, God is making you perfect. You are exactly where God wants you to be.

Life is Fair

"In life you don't always get what you want." Those words still echo in my head. But it's because we desire the wrong things. We want the blessings that other people have. We don't appreciate the things that we do have.

It is only when we want the right thing that we'll get everything we could ever want. Because that is where our hope is found. And Hope does not disappoint.
